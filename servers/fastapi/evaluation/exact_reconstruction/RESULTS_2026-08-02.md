## Remaining measurement risk

OCR output still depends on font rendering, segmentation mode, traineddata family, and raster quality. Keep the engine version, language data hashes, language selection, and page segmentation mode fixed before comparing runs. Pixel/edge scores are sensitive to font rasterization and do not by themselves prove semantic correctness. Stable-ID object IoU is available only when an external extractor or fixture supplies corresponding object boxes.
