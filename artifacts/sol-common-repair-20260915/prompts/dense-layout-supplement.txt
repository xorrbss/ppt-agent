Before writing HTML, compute each nested card's usable height after parent padding, gaps, headings, and fixed media. Budget text lines against that usable height and keep every child within its parent; do not rely on height:auto when the resulting content would exceed the parent. Preserve all required source content, numbers, relationships, and images. Adjust spacing and layout while respecting the existing typography contract and font-size floor; do not introduce a smaller font-size floor. Never use overflow:hidden, clipping, or omission to conceal content that does not fit.
